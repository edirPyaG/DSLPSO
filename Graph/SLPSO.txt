B. Dimensional Sampling Learning Scheme
   The exemplar vector E plays an important role in PSO, determining the direction of evolution. In previous methods, balancing new and existing knowledge has been a challenge. For example, ALC-PSO constructs a potential challenger through a total random update, which may significantly lose existing knowledge, slowing down the convergence. To efficiently update the new exemplar vector based on the old knowledge, a dimensional sampling learning particle swarm optimization (DSL-PSO) is proposed. Different from ALC-PSO, it will build the challengers based on the Gaussian distribution fitted by excellent samples at each dimension. Like LEO, the proposed algorithm possesses reasoning ability in each dimension based on existing knowledge, but it achieves a faster computation than the network-based algorithm. 3) Dynamic Buffer Size Strategy: It is clear that if the value of Θ mentioned above is fixed, the density of effective knowledge within the buffer is difficult to guarantee. Therefore, it is necessary to propose a dynamic buffer size strategy (DBSS) to adjust buffer size adaptively. In DBSS, the buffer size Θ is adjusted within [Θmin, Θmax], where Θmin and Θmax means the upper and lower bound of Θ, respectively. In the early stage of evolution, samples in the buffer just contain a little useful information, which needs to be quickly covered by more efficient knowledge in subsequent iterations. Therefore, the size of the buffer should be smaller at this time. On the other hand, as the evolution progresses, the redundancy in the buffer will become more and more higher so that more effective information can be obtained. Thus, a larger buffer size is necessary along with the convergence of the population. In statistics, the standard value σ represents the density of the distribution of data, and a smaller σ often means a more discrete set. Thus, the value of Θ should decrease along with σ’s growth and increase with σ’s death. Based on this principle, the whole process of dynamic size strategy could be designed as Algorithm 1.
